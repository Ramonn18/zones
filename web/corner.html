People

The camera finds people in front of the corner (Apple Vision, in Zones.app). The mural keeps its light off them, and its drawings move out of their way and gather around them. First, show the camera where the walls are: with everyone out of the picture, calibrate (the projector flashes 12 dots, about 10 seconds).

2. Fit it to the corner

Drag the six pins in the preview: 1 and 6 on the left wall's outer edge, 2 and 5 on the fold, 3 and 4 on the right wall's outer edge. Arrow keys nudge the chosen pin; Shift for more. The grid's squares are 10 cm: when they measure 10 cm on both walls, it fits.

3. The pirate hunt

A chalk-line pirate mural across both walls. Touch the compass, then follow the dotted trail: ship, island, the Kraken, the treasure. Until hands are tracked, the mouse is the hand: hold it on a scene in the preview.

Setups

A setup is the fit, the walls' sizes, the output settings and the mode: save one per room or per day, and bring it back when the projector is set up again.
